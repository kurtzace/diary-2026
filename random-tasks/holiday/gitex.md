Good catch — this changes the location math a lot, because **Expand North Star + LivX are at Expo City Dubai, not Dubai World Trade Centre.** Those are two very different ends of the city.

## Where Citymax Bur Dubai actually sits
- **Citymax Hotel Bur Dubai** is in central/old Dubai (Mankhool/Bur Dubai), nearest Metro around the **ADCB / Al Jafiliya Red Line** area.
- **Expo City Dubai** is way out in **Dubai South (southwest edge)**, next to Al Maktoum airport (DWC) — roughly **35–45 km** from Bur Dubai.

So: **it is not close to the venue.** It's a central-Dubai hotel, not an Expo City one.

## Getting from Citymax to Expo City
- **Metro:** It *is* connected — Red Line to Jebel Ali, then transfer to the **Route 2020 branch** to **Expo City station**. But that's realistically **~55–70 min each way** with the transfer. Cheap (~AED 8–12/trip) but a long daily commute for an 8am–evening event.
- **Taxi/cab:** ~35–50 min off-peak, but **GITEX traffic into Dubai South is brutal** and one-way fares run ~AED 90–120 (₹2,000–2,700). Two round trips = you've eaten most of any package "savings."

## Closer options to target instead
If the priority is minimizing commute to Expo City:
- **Rove Expo City** — basically on-site, the obvious pick (but surges/sells out fast during GITEX).
- Hotels around **Ibn Battuta / Jebel Ali / Dubai South** — much shorter hop on the Route 2020 branch.
- Trade-off: these are in a quieter area with less food/nightlife than Bur Dubai.

## On "cheaper"
Citymax Bur Dubai is a genuinely budget-friendly 3★, so the *room rate* is likely cheap. But for **your** trip the real cost is room + daily commute + time, and on that combined basis a central Citymax is a mediocre fit for an Expo City event.

**My honest read:** the MMT package's Citymax choice is optimized for a generic tourist doing Downtown/Marina sightseeing — not for someone commuting to Expo City every morning. For GITEX at Expo City you want to either (a) stay near Expo City, or (b) accept the ~1hr Metro commute and keep the central hotel for the cheaper rate + better evenings.
