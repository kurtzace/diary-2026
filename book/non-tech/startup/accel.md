## Understanding Startup Funding: Series A, B, C

### What is Startup Funding?

Startup funding is capital investment raised by early-stage companies to fuel growth, product development, team building, and market expansion. Investors (venture capitalists, angel investors, etc.) provide capital in exchange for equity ownership.

### Funding Rounds Explained

**Seed Round**
- Early-stage funding, often from angels, friends & family, or seed funds
- Typically $500K - $2M
- Used for product development and initial team building
- Company is pre-revenue or early revenue

**Series A**
- First institutional venture funding round
- Typically $2M - $15M
- Company has proven product-market fit and early traction
- Used to scale sales, marketing, and operations
- Example: Dreamteam AI raised $53M in Series A with Accel

**Series B**
- Growth capital after Series A success
- Typically $10M - $50M
- Company is scaling revenue and expanding to new markets
- Used for team expansion, infrastructure, and growth initiatives
- Example: Chargebee, Tenable raised $523M, $300M in Series B rounds

**Series C and Beyond**
- Late-stage growth funding
- Typically $50M+
- Company has significant revenue and market presence
- Used for geographic expansion, acquisitions, or IPO preparation
- Example: Urban Company raised $421M before IPO

### Key Metrics at Each Stage

| Stage | Revenue | Valuation | Burn Rate | Runway |
|-------|---------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Seed | $0-$1M | $3M-$10M | Low | 18+ months |
| Series A | $1M-$5M | $10M-$50M | Moderate | 12-18 months |
| Series B | $5M-$20M | $50M-$200M | Higher | 12-18 months |
| Series C | $20M+ | $200M+ | High | 18+ months |
