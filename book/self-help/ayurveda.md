### systems 

• Agni: The body's digestive fire key to wellness.
• Ojas: Vital energy and substance for immunity.
• Malas: Body waste products like sweat and urine.
• Ama: Toxins caused by undigested food or stress.
• Dhatus: Body tissues nourished by the food we eat


• Prana: The vital life energy that is both physical and spiritual.

• Srotamsi: The internal body channels through which life energy and substances (such as blood) flow.

• Elements and Qualities: The universe and human body are built from five elements—ether, air, fire, water, and earth—each carrying specific qualities.

• The Doshas: Biological energies formed from combinations of these elements, divided into:

	• Vata dosha: The dosha of movement (associated with space and air).

	• Pitta dosha: The dosha of transformation (associated with fire and water).

	• Kapha dosha: The dosha of structure and substance (associated with water and earth).

• Prakruti: The unique physical and mental proportion of the three doshas that defines an individual.
